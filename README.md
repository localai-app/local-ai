# Local AI

A private AI assistant that runs entirely on your own Windows PC. Your chats, files and memories stay on your computer;
it only goes online to search the web for you or to download the AI model you choose.

## Download

Get the latest installer from **[Releases](https://github.com/localai-app/local-ai/releases/latest)**
(`LocalAI-Setup-….exe`), run it, and follow the setup screens. Installing doesn't need administrator rights.

This preview isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose **More info**, then
**Run anyway**.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- Best with a graphics card that has 8 GB or more of memory (it also runs on the processor alone, just slowly)
- About 1 GB for the program plus 3–9 GB for the AI model you pick during setup

## What it can do

- Chat with a local AI model that setup picks for your PC (Qwen3.5, Apache-2.0), or use your own `.gguf` model
- Read and search your own files, and create or change text files only after you click **Save**
- Search the web and show its sources
- Remember things you tell it, which you can view, edit or delete
- Optional: create and edit images, chat from your phone on your home Wi-Fi, and answer questions in your Discord server

## Updates

Local AI checks this page once a day and shows **Update available** when there's a new version.
